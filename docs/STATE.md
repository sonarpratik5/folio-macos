## Selection Insights (2026-11-14 follow-up)

The human clarified that missing Insights meant selected-text explanations. The reader toolbar now has a labelled Insights toggle (I; V restores plain selection), also reachable from the right rail and Assistant menu. Enabling alone never sends. Completing a mouse or Shift-arrow selection while enabled sends at most 12K selected characters and bounded paper context to the chosen provider. A native transient popup anchored to the first selected line shows the passage, page, concise explanation with citations, Stop, retry, Copy and Ask more. It fits short responses and caps the response area at 260 points with scrolling for overflow. The backdrop uses translucent thin material, with a solid background for Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast. Enabling Insights and selecting text leave the existing sidebar unchanged. Outside click, Escape or Close dismisses the popup; Escape/Close retain the selection mode. Ask more opens Ask with the passage attached, without sending. New selections cancel prior work; repeated identical selections are deduplicated. Changing annotation tool, leaving the reader, closing the insight popup or changing provider clears/cancels the selection request. Plain selection never calls a provider. Responses are ephemeral and do not create chat threads. Selection explanations read the managed PDF independently of saved assistant history, so unreadable or stale chat/overview files do not block them and remain untouched. Paper at a glance stays exclusively on the library Insights/overview page, reachable from the reader's Paper overview icon.

Selection requests include a local surrounding window of at most 1,600 characters when a short selection can be located in extracted page text. The stable paper map stays capped at 12K characters. Search/page tools can retrieve evidence across the supplied PDF, and web search is available when needed or requested; neither whole-paper reads nor browsing is automatic. Completed insight answers reuse a process-local cache (24 results), keyed by PDF fingerprint, page, selection, provider and model. No cache is persisted; retry bypasses it. Scans, extraction truncation and tool limits still apply.

- OpenReview authentication reads local dotenv/launch-environment token and login credentials; token-first, lazy username/password sign-in, and one login/retry after 401/403. No automatic requests on opening Peer Reviews.

- App bundles contain no build-recorded dotenv path. Finder/Applications launches resolve `~/Library/Application Support/FolioMac/.env` for the running user, with environment precedence and development/legacy Keychain fallbacks. ## Logic recovery corrections — 2026-11-15
